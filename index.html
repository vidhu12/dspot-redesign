Which shops can I order from?

Any shop that delivers to a Gurugram address. Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Meesho and Nykaa all work, along with smaller online stores.

How does D-Spot know my parcel is mine?

Add the order in the app after you check out. Upload a screenshot of the order and the app reads the shop from it, so we can match the parcel when it lands.

When can I book a delivery slot?

As soon as the parcel reaches D-Spot. We message you when it arrives and you pick Morning, Afternoon, Evening or Night.

Can I change the slot after booking?

Yes. You can change it any time until the parcel goes out for delivery. If a slot fills up, the app asks you to pick another.

Do I have to share my location?

No. You can search for your building or type the address. Dropping a pin is optional.

How do I reach someone?

Chat on WhatsApp, call or email. We're there 8 AM to 11 PM for anything about an order or a delivery.
